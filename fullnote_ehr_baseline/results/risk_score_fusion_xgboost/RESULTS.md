# EHR + FullNote risk-score XGBoost results

This experiment compresses each 768-dimensional FullNote embedding into one
logistic-regression mortality risk score and appends that score to the 4,041 EHR
features.

## Leakage control

- Five `StratifiedGroupKFold` folds grouped by `subject_id`
- 31,792 training admissions received exactly one out-of-fold score
- 25,852 unique training patients
- Zero train/holdout patient overlap in every fold
- Final note-risk classifier fitted on all training embeddings only
- Validation and test scores generated by the final training-only classifier
- XGBoost candidate and threshold selected with validation only

## Note-only risk classifier

## Comparison with direct concatenation

Risk-score fusion increases test recall from 31.46% to 48.60% and F1 from
0.3819 to 0.4010. Direct concatenation retains better AUROC, AUPRC, precision,
specificity, accuracy, Brier score, and log loss. The high selected threshold and
probability losses show that the class-weighted risk-fusion XGBoost output is not
well calibrated as an absolute mortality probability.
